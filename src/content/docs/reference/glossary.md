---
title: Glossary
description: Plain-English meanings of the words Engram uses, from cloze to vault.
order: 4
---

**Again, Hard, Good, Easy**  
The four ratings you give a card after seeing its answer. They tell Engram how well you remembered it. See [Study](/docs/features/study/#rating-a-card).

**Backlink**  
A link to the current note from some other note. Shown on the right of the Notes screen.

**Card**  
One flashcard, with a front (the prompt) and a back (the answer).

**Cloze**  
A card that hides part of a sentence for you to fill in, written as `{{c1::hidden words}}`. See [Decks and cards](/docs/features/decks-and-cards/#cloze-cards).

**Deck**  
A group of cards. Decks can be nested with `::`, like `Japanese::N5`.

**Due**  
A card is due when it's time to review it again. The **Due** counter in the top bar shows how many cards are waiting today.

**Explain**  
A free, offline summary of a note. See [Explain](/docs/features/explain/).

**Focus label**  
Your own word for what a profile is about, like a subject or course. Labels don't change how anything works.

**FSRS**  
The scheduling method Engram uses to decide when each card comes back. Short for Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler.

**Learning**  
A new or forgotten card that's still going through its short first steps (minutes apart) before it moves to longer gaps.

**Pack**  
A theme. Engram has seven built-in packs, and you can make your own. See [Themes](/docs/features/themes/).

**Profile**  
A separate workspace with its own notes, cards, theme and timers. See [Profiles](/docs/getting-started/profiles/).

**Reduce effects**  
A setting that turns off blur, motion and background gradients for a calmer, faster interface.

**Spaced repetition**  
Reviewing things at growing intervals, just before you'd forget them. It's one of the most reliable ways to remember things long term.

**Suspend**  
Take a card out of study without deleting it. Suspended cards show as HOLD.

**Tutor**  
The optional AI chat assistant. Free, no subscription, but it needs an AI provider. See [AI tutor](/docs/features/tutor/).

**Vault**  
The folder where a profile's notes and database are kept. See [Your data](/docs/reference/your-data/).

**Wikilink**  
A link between notes written in double square brackets, like `[[Particles]]`.
